Case 01 • Payments & Fintech

Google Pay Gifting Vouchers

Bridging India's cultural Moi / Shagun cash envelope tradition into UPI through dual-voucher architecture, P2M kirana redemption, and zero-breakage rollover.

Google Pay

• The Unmet Problem

While UPI handles billions of monthly transactions with zero friction, it remains completely sterile for gifting. Sending ₹501 for Pongal or Diwali feels identical to paying an electricity bill.

  • No emotional ceremonial moment: Transactional coldness degrades traditional shagun gifting.
  • Single-brand lock-in: Amazon/Swiggy gift cards force recipients into merchants they may not want (30%+ industry breakage).
  • Loss anxiety: Senders worry unspent cash transfers or forgotten gift cards vanish forever.

• User Personas

Designed across three interdependent participants in the Indian retail economy:

  • The Sender (Urban Professional): Wants to send meaningful festival/function money to relatives without brand restriction.
  • The Receiver (Family / Elder): Often less digital-native; requires an effortless, delightful scratch-and-claim experience.
  • The Merchant (Kirana QR Owner): Local mom-and-pop stores already displaying UPI QR codes who gain instant footfall.

Dual-Voucher Architecture & Ecosystem Flow

1. Money Voucher (Cash-like Envelope)

  • Capped Gifting: ₹50 to ₹5,000 to maintain gift nature and avoid AML evasion.
  • Scratch Card Moment: Receiver unpacks greeting card with custom festive animations.
  • 7-Day Auto-Refund: If unclaimed within 7 days, 100% of funds refund back to sender's bank account automatically.

2. Gift Voucher (Multi-Brand Choice)

  • Redeemable Everywhere: Usable across partner merchants (Zomato, Myntra, Swiggy, offline retail).
  • Zero-Breakage Rollover: Day 90 reminder; if unspent, rolls 100% into Google Play / Wallet balance.
  • Family Circle Loop: Shared family QR scans generate points toward free gift voucher credits.

Case 02 • AI Evaluation & Vernacular NLP

Google Search Campus Guild

Crowdsourcing hyper-local Indian code-mixed slang (Tanglish, Hinglish) and regional nuances through an accredited college student micro-annotation flywheel.

Search & Gemini

• The Vernacular AI Blindspot

State-of-the-art LLMs (Gemini, GPT) repeatedly fail on colloquial Indian internet communication. Gen Z and millennial Indian users constantly code-switch between English and regional tongues (Tanglish, Hinglish, Tenglish).

  • Formal training bias: Classical datasets rely on textbook Tamil or Hindi, missing contemporary slang.
  • High annotation cost: Formal linguistics agencies cost \$20–\$45/hour and cannot keep pace with dynamic youth trends.
  • Hallucinations on regional queries: AI summaries give tone-deaf or completely incorrect interpretations of colloquial intent.

• The Campus Solution

Mobilizing the exact demographic who invents and evolves vernacular slang: college students across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 Indian universities.

  • 10-15 Micro-Tasks Daily: 30-second conversational reviews conducted directly in campus portal.
  • Triple-Blind Consensus: 3 to 5 independent students evaluate the same query; consensus threshold ≥85%.
  • Gold-Standard Honeypots: Embedded benchmark questions instantly flag bots, random clicks, and coord gaming.

Non-Cash Academic Flywheel & Economics

Rather than introducing financial friction or transactional micro-payments, Campus Guild operates on high-value academic and career capital:

1. Verified Credentials

Official "Google AI Language Fellow" digital badges and milestone certificates shareable on LinkedIn and CVs.

2. Campus Leaderboards

College-vs-college competitions giving recognition to student clubs, tech chapters, and department ambassadors.

3. Career Fast-Track

Top 1% high-accuracy contributors unlock priority screening and fast-track interviews for Google India internships.

North Star Metric

Agreement-checked corrections per active student per week. Ensures both volume and multi-party alignment quality.

Operational Metrics

Gold-question pass rate (>94%); 30-day student retention (>65%); Error-rate drop on fixed vernacular evaluation test suite.

Integrity Guardrails

Collusion detection across common IP subnets; Student daily workload cap (maximum 20 minutes/day) to prevent burnout.

Case 03 • Short-Video UX & Creator Commerce

YouTube Shorts Smart Bar & Products Drawer

Eliminating visual clutter on the 9:16 mobile canvas while establishing frictionless creator affiliate commerce and a personalized, user-steered feed.

YouTube Shorts

• The 3 Pain Points

  • Severe Visual Clutter: The vertical 9:16 frame has 7+ overlay icons (Like, Dislike, Comment, Share, Remix, Sound, Menu) covering ~25% of the right screen edge. The buried "Clear Screen" feature proves users hate the clutter.
  • Weak Creator Commerce Conversion: Viewers loving a creator's outfit or gadget must pause, navigate to description comments, and hunt for third-party links, losing the session.
  • Rigid Algorithmic Stream: Users cannot easily steer their immediate mood or topic (e.g., Tech vs Food vs Regional Comedy) without leaving the immersive feed.

• The 3 Solution Pillars

  • 1. Smart Bar (Clean Thumb Zone): Relocates primary actions (Home, Comments, Create +, Search, More Sheet) cleanly to the bottom ergonomic thumb zone. Double-tap likes the video; swipe closes overlays.
  • 2. Products Drawer: Subtle, high-intent product pill emerges under title only when creator tags or visual recognition matches merchandise. Two taps to view and checkout without killing playback.
  • 3. Dynamic Personal Top Bar: Reorderable topic chips (Tech, Cooking, Tamil Nadu, Gaming) allowing instantaneous feed pivoting.

Case 04 • Real-time Video & B2B2C Entertainment

Google Meet Couple Pack

Official DRM-compliant OTT synchronized co-watching with smart auto-audio ducking, floating PiP face bubbles, and paired platform subscriptions.

Google Meet

• The Co-Watching Dilemma

Millions of long-distance couples, students, and migrant families (e.g. across Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai) try to watch movies together over video calls, but current workflows are fundamentally broken:

  • DRM Screen Blackouts: Screen sharing Widevine/FairPlay protected video results in a black box.
  • Echo & Feedback: Movie audio from phone/laptop speakers feeds back into microphones, destroying intimacy.
  • Low Non-Work Meet Usage: Meet is treated as a sterile 9-to-5 enterprise tool, missing leisure social hours.

• The B2B2C Solution

Instead of Google charging users per movie, Google Meet partners with OTTs (Disney+ Hotstar, Prime, SonyLIV, Zee5) via official SDKs:

  • Dual-Token Playback Sync: Both phones/tvs stream independently from OTT CDN; sync latency <150ms.
  • Intelligent Audio Ducking: When either partner speaks, movie audio ducks -15dB instantly so dialogue is never missed.
  • Round Floating Video Bubble: Partner's face stays connected without obscuring widescreen cinematic subtitles.
