# Google Product Strategy Cases

Four independent product proposals by Ashokkumar S.
Concept work only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

| # | Case | Problem | Idea |
|---|------|---------|------|
| 1 | Google Pay Gifting Vouchers | UPI has no gifting moment; gift cards lock users to one brand | Money Voucher + Gift Voucher, redeemable at verified merchants |
| 2 | Search Campus Guild | AI misreads regional languages and slang | Students review and correct AI answers, earn certificates |
| 3 | YouTube Shorts Smart Bar | Cluttered screen, weak commerce, rigid feed | Clean screen, Products drawer, personal top bar |
| 4 | Google Meet Couple Pack | Co-watching OTT over screen share breaks | OTT-branded Couple Pack with Meet as the co-watch layer |
